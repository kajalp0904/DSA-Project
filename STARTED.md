## 🚀 What to Do Next

### 1. Open Your Browser
Go to: **http://localhost:3000**

### 2. Create Your Account
- Click the **"Login"** button in the top-right
- Click **"Don't have an account? Sign up"**
- Fill in:
  - Your name
  - Email address
  - Password (minimum 6 characters)
- Click **"Sign Up"**

### 3. Complete Your Profile
After logging in:
- Click **"Profile"** in the sidebar
- Click **"Edit Profile"**
- Add:
  - Bio
  - Location (e.g., "New York, USA")
  - College/University (e.g., "MIT")
  - Interests (e.g., "Coding", "Music", "Travel")
- Click **"Save Changes"**

### 4. Discover Friends!
- Click **"Discover"** in the sidebar
- See friend recommendations based on:
  - Mutual friends
  - Shared interests
  - Same college/location
- Use filters to search by location, interest, or college
- Click **"Connect"** on people you'd like to be friends with

### 5. Manage Connections
- Click **"Connections"** in the sidebar
- View:
  - **Friends tab** - All your friends
  - **Requests tab** - Pending friend requests
- Click **"Accept Request"** to accept requests

## 📊 How Friend Recommendations Work

The system calculates a **friend score** for each person:
```
score = (mutual_friends × 2) + (shared_interests × 3) + location_bonus + college_bonus
```

Then shows the **top 10 best matches**!
