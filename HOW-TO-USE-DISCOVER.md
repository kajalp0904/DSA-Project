# 🔍 How to Discover and Connect with People

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Login/Create Account
1. Go to http://localhost:3000
2. Click **"Login"** button (top-right)
3. Click **"Don't have an account? Sign up"**
4. Fill in:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Password (min 6 characters)
5. Click **"Sign Up"**

✅ You're now logged in!

---

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile (Important!)

Before discovering people, **complete your profile** for better recommendations:

1. Click **"Profile"** in the sidebar (👤 icon)
2. Click **"Edit Profile"** button
3. Fill in your information:
   - **Bio:** Write a short description about yourself
   - **Location:** e.g., "New York, USA" or "London, UK"
   - **College:** e.g., "MIT", "Harvard", "NYU"
   - **Interests:** Click **"Add"** for each interest
     - Examples: "Coding", "Music", "Travel", "Cooking", "Photography", "Sports"
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Why this matters:** The algorithm matches you based on these details!

---

### Step 3: Go to Discover Page

1. In the sidebar, click **"Discover"** (🔍 icon)
2. Or click **"Discover"** in the top navigation

---

### Step 4: See Your Recommendations

You'll see cards showing people recommended for you:

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│        [Profile Pic]        │
│         John Doe            │
│     📍 New York, USA       │
│     🎓 MIT                 │
│   👥 3 mutual friends       │
│   ✨ 5 shared interests    │
│                             │
│  #Coding #Music #Travel     │
│                             │
│      [Connect Button]       │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### Step 5: Use Filters (Optional)

At the top of the Discover page, you can filter:

#### Filter by Location
- Type a city: "New York"
- Type a country: "USA"
- Type a state: "California"

#### Filter by Interest
- Type an interest: "Coding"
- See only people who share that interest

#### Filter by College
- Type a college: "MIT"
- See only people from that college

💡 **Tip:** You can use multiple filters at once!

---

### Step 6: Connect with People

1. Click the **"Connect"** button on someone's card
2. A friend request is sent to them
3. They'll see your request in their "Connections" page
4. When they accept, you become friends!

---

### Step 7: Check Your Requests

1. Click **"Connections"** in the sidebar (👥 icon)
2. You'll see two tabs:
   - **Friends:** All your current friends
   - **Requests:** People who want to be friends with you
3. Click **"Accept Request"** to approve a pending request

---

## 🎯 Understanding the Recommendations

### How Recommendations Work

The system scores each person using this algorithm:

```
Score = (Mutual Friends × 2) + (Shared Interests × 3)
+ Location Match + College Match
```

**Example:**
- 3 mutual friends = 6 points
- 2 shared interests = 6 points  
- Same college = 1 point
- **Total Score = 13**

The **top 10 highest scores** are shown to you!

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Complete Your Profile First**
   - More details = better recommendations
   
2. **Add Multiple Interests**
   - More interests = more matches
   
3. **Use Filters to Narrow Down**
   - Find people in specific locations
   - Find people with specific hobbies
   
4. **Check Regularly**
   - New people join, new recommendations appear
   
5. **Be Active**
   - Accept friend requests promptly
   - Send requests to interesting people

## 📊 What Makes a Good Match?

### High Score Indicators:
✅ 5+ mutual friends  
✅ 3+ shared interests  
✅ Same location  
✅ Same college  
✅ Similar bio keywords  

### When to Connect:
- You share common interests
- You have mutual friends
- They're from the same location/college
- Their profile looks interesting
